How it works

You give the orders. Your Macs carry them out.

Your Blowhorn account keeps your queue, profiles and history in sync. The work itself happens in the desktop app on machines you own.

  1. 01

    Write it once

    Draft the post in the Mac app, from the Python CLI, or let your agent draft it. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

    {{ partial "code-card.html" (dict "code" "blowhorn post --check-queue --profile all\n" "title" "terminal" "lang" "bash" "prompt" true) }}

  2. 02

    Pick the moment

    Queue it for now or for launch o'clock. Add the reposts, quotes, comments and reactions your team will send after it. Preview any run first.

    {{ partial "code-card.html" (dict "code" "blowhorn post --profile all --dry-run\n" "title" "terminal" "lang" "bash" "prompt" true) }}

  3. 03

    It goes out as you

    Your Mac does the typing and clicking in your own Chrome, at a human pace, signed in as you. Every result lands in Activity.
