Trust

Yours to keep

Many public voices, one coordinated queue, every action on your own Mac.

  • Every action happens on your own Mac

    Blowhorn never acts for you from a server. The app on your Mac does the work, in your own Chrome where a platform needs it (see the privacy page).

  • One queue and one set of credentials, on any Mac

    Your queue, profiles, run history and results stay in sync in your account. Add any number of Macs, yours or your teammates', to one fleet: coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two of them. See where your data lives.

  • Hacker News: submit and comment only

    Blowhorn submits stories and comments. It never votes, and caps each profile at 2 link submissions a day. Post to Hacker News.

Control

You're in control

Built for AI. Controlled by you.

  • Will I get banned?

    Blowhorn works at a human pace you choose and never solves a CAPTCHA for you.

  • Will it post twice?

    Your Macs coordinate through one queue, so the same post isn't sent from two of them.

  • What if an AI agent is driving?

    Agents get the same queue and limits you do. Only approved posts go out.
