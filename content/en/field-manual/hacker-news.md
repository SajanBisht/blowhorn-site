## What Hacker News rewards

- **Curiosity.** "It's ok to post your own stuff part of the time, but the primary use of the site should be for curiosity" ([HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html)).
- **The original source.** "If a post reports on something found on another site, submit the latter" (same source).
- **Humans talking.** "HN is for conversation between humans" (same source).

## What Hacker News penalizes

- **Asking for votes or comments.** "Don't solicit upvotes, comments, or submissions." Votes and comments should come from people who find a post interesting, not from a promotion push (same source).
- **Promotion as a habit.** "Please don't use HN primarily for promotion" (same source).
- **Generated text.** "Don't post generated text or AI-edited text" (same source).
- **Delete and repost.** "Please don't delete and repost" (same source).

## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** submit things you'd read yourself, with the original title.
- **Do** write every comment yourself, and stay for the discussion.
- **Don't** tell anyone to go vote, not even your team.
- **Don't** submit your own links every day.
- <span class="hypothesis">Hypothesis:</span> HN's ranking discounts votes that arrive from one shared link or one group at once. Widely believed; HN doesn't publish its ranking.

## What Blowhorn does on Hacker News

Blowhorn submits links and text posts and comments from your own browser. It never votes: there's nothing to configure and no way to turn it on. It allows at most 2 link submissions per profile per UTC day and fails a title over 80 characters rather than cutting it ([Platforms](/docs/reference/platforms/)). Every submission is permanent, so dry-run first.
