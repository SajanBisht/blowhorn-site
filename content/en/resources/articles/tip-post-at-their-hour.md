title: "Post at your audience's hour, not yours"
summary: "Write whenever you think clearly; post when your readers are actually scrolling. Scheduling exists to separate those two moments, and a one-hour shift in posting time is the cheapest experiment in this whole series."
date: 2026-10-27

The moment you write a post is the moment it is convenient for you. Your audience reads on their own schedule: the morning commute, the lunch scroll, the evening wind-down. A post fired at your convenience lands in an empty room.

Pick the troop's posting times from the readers' day, not the writer's, and keep a genuine quiet period overnight. Then change one troop's hour and watch its results for a cycle before moving the others. Timing experiments are cheap and the differences are usually not subtle.
