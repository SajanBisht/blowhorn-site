Judging a schedule by one post is like judging a diet by one meal. Set a five-minute weekly review instead: scan the week's activities and ask three questions. Did posts land when they were scheduled? What failed, and do the reasons share a cause? Which post earned a response, and what was different about it?

Write down the one change you will make, and only one. Next week's review starts by checking whether it worked. That small loop, repeated, is the entire method behind the Product intelligence series.
