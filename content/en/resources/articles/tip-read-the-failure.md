---
title: "Read the failure before you retry"
summary: "Every failed post carries its reason, and the reason names the fix. Retrying without reading it repeats the same failure at the same target, and teaches the platform you are not paying attention."
date: 2026-10-27
author: "Blowhorn team"
layout: article
tags: ["tips", "reading-results"]
---

A failed post is a message, not just a mark. "Target gone" means fix the list. "Not signed in" means fix the session. "Platform refused" means the post or the pace broke a rule. Three reasons, three different repairs, and none of them is "try again".

Make it a habit: no retry until the reason has been read and the cause fixed. A queue will repeat a mistake at scale if you let it, and a platform watching the same failure arrive five times draws its own conclusions.
