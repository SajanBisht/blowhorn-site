---
title: "A platform limit is information, not an error"
summary: "When a platform slows or refuses your posts, it is telling you the current price of attention. The right response is a smaller, slower run - never a faster retry."
date: 2026-10-28
author: "Blowhorn team"
layout: article
tags: ["tips", "platform-rules"]
---

A limit reached is a platform telling you, precisely, where its boundary sits today. That is useful information. Treat it the way you would treat a price: adjust what you buy. Halve the batch, widen the spacing, and let the troop finish smaller and slower.

What never helps is pushing: retrying at the same pace converts a soft limit into a hard one, and hard limits come with longer memories. Accounts rarely get in trouble for the post that hit the limit. They get in trouble for the twenty that followed it.
