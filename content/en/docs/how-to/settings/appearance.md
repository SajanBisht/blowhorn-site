title: "Change how the app looks"
description: "Pick the app's theme, make the side rail and the + button solid or tinted glass, and choose whether the desktop shows through."

The **appearance** card in Settings holds the app's theme, what the side rail
and the round **+** button are made of, and how roomy the screens are. Every
change applies at once, in every Blowhorn window, and is kept for the next
launch.

## Choose a theme

1. Open **Settings** from the account menu.
2. In the **appearance** card, pick a **theme**: **System** follows your
   Mac's Light or Dark setting, **Night** is a warmer, dimmer dark for late
   hours, and **Day** is a higher-contrast light for bright rooms.

## Make the rail and the + button glass

1. In the **appearance** card, pick a **material**:
   - **Solid** - the plain rail and a solid **+** button.
   - **Teal glass** - the default. A teal tint over the rail and a teal glass
     button.
   - **Ink glass** - a dark, smoky tint, with a white **+**.
   - **Clear glass** - an untinted pane, with a black **+**.
2. Under a glass material, move **tint strength** to set how much of the
   glass's colour lies over the rail.

**reduce transparency** is on to start with, so the glass stays opaque: the
rail is a solid tinted pane and the **+** button a solid tinted disc.

## Let the desktop show through

1. Pick **Teal glass**, **Ink glass** or **Clear glass**.
2. Turn **reduce transparency** off. The desktop now shows through the rail,
   softened, the way it does in Finder's sidebar, and the screen behind the
   **+** button shows through it.
3. Move **see-through** to set how much of the desktop shows through the
   rail.

The screens beside the rail always stay opaque, so their text never depends
on your wallpaper. Each theme and material stops **tint strength** and
**see-through** where the rail's text would get hard to read over a busy or
very light or dark desktop, so the end of a slider can move when you change
the theme. Links on a glass rail, such as **open the log folder**, take a
deeper or paler shade of teal than elsewhere, with a dotted underline, so they
stay as readable as the rest of the rail.

When your Mac's own **Reduce transparency** is on (System Settings >
Accessibility > Display), Blowhorn keeps the glass opaque whatever this card
says, and the card tells you so. The desktop showing through the rail is a
macOS feature; on any other system the glass stays opaque.

## Change the spacing

In the **appearance** card, pick **spacing**: **Comfortable** is the default,
**Compact** fits more rows, and **Spacious** makes text and targets about 25%
larger.
