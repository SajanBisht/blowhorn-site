# Instagram

**Coming soon.** Instagram is not generally available yet.

Early work for Instagram exists in the app, and it is not ready for your
posts. It works in your own signed-in Chrome, never from a server. What
exists today:

- **You sign in by hand.** Sign in to Instagram yourself in your own Chrome
  profile. Blowhorn never fills a sign-in form, never retries a failed
  sign-in and never runs without a visible browser. A sign-in wall,
  challenge or captcha stops Instagram for that profile and asks you to sign
  in.
- **A dry run comes first.** A dry run checks a post and shows the steps a
  live post would take, without touching the browser.
- **Limits apply.** Posts to a personal account. Every post needs an image or a video, since Instagram has no text-only post. A caption is at most 2,200 characters, with at most 30 hashtags and 20 mentions, and an image's shape must be between 4:5 and 1.91:1. Blowhorn stops at 3 posts per profile per day. Business and Creator accounts are a later phase.
- **The credential** Blowhorn checks is `IG_USERNAME`. It names your account; it
  is never a password.

Nothing has been confirmed against the real Instagram yet. Every reading
Blowhorn takes there is unverified until a first hand-supervised live post
confirms it, so don't rely on Instagram for real posts until this page says
otherwise.
