# YouTube

**Coming soon.** YouTube is not generally available yet.

Early work for YouTube exists in the app, and it is not ready for your
posts. It works in your own signed-in Chrome, never from a server. What
exists today:

- **You sign in by hand.** Sign in to YouTube yourself in your own Chrome
  profile. Blowhorn never fills a sign-in form, never retries a failed
  sign-in and never runs without a visible browser. A sign-in wall,
  challenge or captcha stops YouTube for that profile and asks you to sign
  in.
- **A dry run comes first.** A dry run checks a post and shows the steps a
  live post would take, without touching the browser.
- **Limits apply.** Posts a video with a title and caption. A caption is at most 5,000 characters, and Blowhorn stops at 3 posts per profile per day.
- **The credential** Blowhorn checks is `YT_CHANNEL_URL`, the link to your channel. It names your account; it
  is never a password.

Nothing has been confirmed against the real YouTube yet. Every reading
Blowhorn takes there is unverified until a first hand-supervised live post
confirms it, so don't rely on YouTube for real posts until this page says
otherwise.
