# Facebook

**Coming soon.** Facebook is not generally available yet.

Early work for Facebook exists in the app, and it is not ready for your
posts. It works in your own signed-in Chrome, never from a server. What
exists today:

- **You sign in by hand.** Sign in to Facebook yourself in your own Chrome
  profile. Blowhorn never fills a sign-in form, never retries a failed
  sign-in and never runs without a visible browser. A sign-in wall,
  challenge or captcha stops Facebook for that profile and asks you to sign
  in.
- **A dry run comes first.** A dry run checks a post and shows the steps a
  live post would take, without touching the browser.
- **Limits apply.** Posts to a personal profile. A post is at most 63,206 characters. Blowhorn holds each profile to 3 posts, 20 reactions, 10 comments, 5 shares, 5 friend requests and 5 messages a day.
- **The credential** Blowhorn checks is `FB_PROFILE_URL`, the link to your profile. It names your account; it
  is never a password.

Nothing has been confirmed against the real Facebook yet. Every reading
Blowhorn takes there is unverified until a first hand-supervised live post
confirms it, so don't rely on Facebook for real posts until this page says
otherwise.
